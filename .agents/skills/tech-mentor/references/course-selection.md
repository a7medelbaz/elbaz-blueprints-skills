# Course selection

Treat courses as curriculum components, not a link dump. For each selected course or segment record:

- what it teaches and its version/status;
- the learner gap it closes;
- prerequisites and sequence position;
- effort, cost, and access constraints;
- `required`, `recommended`, or `optional` status;
- practical outcome and proof of completion;
- risks such as outdated content, duplication, broad scope, missing practice, or version mismatch.

Prefer a small complementary set. Link upstream documentation for changing details instead of copying manuals. A course is not evidence of competence unless paired with an observable task, explanation, test, review, or portfolio artifact.
